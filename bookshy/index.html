The Alchemist

Paulo Coelho

The story follows Santiago, a young shepherd boy from Spain who keeps having a dream about a treasure hidden near the Egyptian pyramids. Inspired by this recurring dream and with encouragement from a mysterious king, he sets off on a journey to find his Personal Legend (meaning: his life's purpose). Along the way, Santiago meets many people — a crystal merchant, an Englishman studying alchemy, a wise alchemist, and a desert girl he falls in love with. Through each of these encounters, he learns valuable lessons about life, faith, love, and destiny.
